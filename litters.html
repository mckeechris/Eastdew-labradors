When the time is right

Quality before quantity.

Eastdew does not pre-announce speculative litters. A waiting list opens only when a pregnancy is confirmed.

After puppies arrive, we take time to assess the litter before matching puppies to prospective homes.

The Eastdew process

01 · Confirmed

Pregnancy confirmed. Waiting list opens and prospective owners can tell us whether they are hoping for a dog or bitch.

02 · Arrived

Puppies arrive. We see what nature has produced and begin watching their individual development.

03 · Matched

We contact prospective owners and make careful matches based on the puppy and the home.
