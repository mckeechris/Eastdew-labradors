About Eastdew

A story of passion, place and purpose.

The home, the dogs and the philosophy behind Eastdew.

Where it began

The beginning of Eastdew

Our journey with Labradors began in 2017 with Farnfield Cassiopeia, our foundation bitch. From that moment, Eastdew was born.

Built on a love for the breed and a belief that health, temperament and type must always come first.

The Eastdew vision

We breed for the future.

For health, soundness and the true Labrador temperament.

The future is not about producing more Labradors.

It is about producing better ones.

Where we are today

Today

Eastdew remains small by design, with Candy, Comet and Nova at the heart of the current story.

Our aim is to keep improving while protecting the qualities that make the Labrador the breed we love.
